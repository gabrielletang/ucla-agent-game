`gen9.json` is a compressed extract (types, base stats, abilities, move data, type chart, natures)
of Pokémon Showdown's Gen 9 data tables (https://github.com/smogon/pokemon-showdown, MIT License),
taken from the copy bundled with poke-env 0.17.0 (https://github.com/hsahovic/poke-env, MIT License).
